# UFO site

A small multi-page static site. Enter a mix of funds, pick a benchmark (SPY by default), see the result side by side. Runs in the browser; nothing is uploaded; no server, no analytics, no external resources.

## Page map
Each page is `folder/index.html` with relative links, so it works under any sub-path.
- `index.html`: Home (M14). The hero asks "Is your mix keeping up?" and its card answers it for the sample household: the question, then the honest answer ("Yes." / "Not quite." / "Neck and neck." from `heroAnswer()` in `js/result.mjs`, so it reads right whichever way prices move), the gap in dollars, then "Would yours?" (issue 181). The main button is "Check your own mix" (`try/?own=1`). Hero card: a **live preview of the sample household** (computed at load by `js/home.mjs` from the same prices and the same `js/result.mjs` code as Try, so the numbers always match), the **Plus section** (`#plus`: a phone screenshot of the real Plus demo, light and dark, linking to `plus-demo/`), and one privacy line linking to Privacy. Issue 239 cut the copy: say each thing once per page ("See Plus with a sample household" is the hero's second button; the Plus section's one button is "Join Plus early access"; Teach is reached from the footer).
- `try/`: the tool. Headline with return and Sharpe for both, one chart, then a **result-aware next step** (behind/ahead/level, from `nextStep()` in `js/result.mjs`) pointing to Plus, the Plus teaser, "Try your own mix" and the lesson. After a sample (or with `?own=1`, Home's "Check your own mix") a **"Now check your own mix"** panel offers two paths: type a few funds (opens the mix editor on the first fund) or drop a positions file, with a one-line how-to for the formats `js/positions.mjs` reads (Fidelity's positions download and the symbol,value template) (issue 184). "Show details" (full metrics, month table), "Your mix and settings" (mix editor, benchmark, history, style, deposits) and "Advanced: use your own price history (CSV)" are disclosures. The price date is a small plain line under the chart, not a highlighted chip (issue 225). `?sample=1` loads the sample portfolio.
- `learn/`: lesson index. `learn/sharpe/`: a beginner lesson (M14). It opens with the two Sharpe ratios from Try and "What in the world is that?", then five marked-up steps (circled numbers, highlighter, margin notes, one focus number per card), ends with "So what?" in one sentence, and offers four optional "Going further" cards on portfolio theory. It runs on the mix last loaded on Try in this tab (kept in `sessionStorage`), else the sample, through the same `runResult()` as Try.
- `about/`: the founder (photo `img/founder.jpg`, name and LinkedIn from `site.json` `founder`; an empty `linkedin` hides the link), how it works, limits, not advice, and the legal name.
- `teach/` (instructor session plan, pilot request; not in the top navigation since it is for instructors only: reached from "For instructors" in the footer), `pricing/` (from `data/plans.json`), `signup/` (waitlist form; `?plan=plus` preselects Plus, School shows only for students and instructors), `privacy/`, `terms/` (each shows a "Last updated" date).
- **Config (M13, M14):** `data/site.json` (name "Ur Family Office", wordmark "UFO", `base_url`, tagline "A higher view of your money", `legal_name`, logo, contact email, `founder`, `signup_endpoint`, `checkout_urls`). `js/chrome.mjs` renders the header and footer on every page from it; `js/config.mjs`, `js/plans.mjs`, `js/signup.mjs` hold the testable logic (`tests/site/config.test.mjs`). A plan's `checkout_url` (plans.json, or `checkout_urls` in site.json) turns its button into a payment link; sign-ups post to Formspree (`signup_endpoint`, https only): exactly email, role, school, plan and consent plus the honeypot `_gotcha`, as JSON with `Accept: application/json`, reading the JSON reply (`submitSignup()` and `signupOutcome()` in `js/signup.mjs`; no Formspree or reCAPTCHA script is loaded; tests use a mock fetch and never post to the real form). With the endpoint emptied the form sends nothing; if `contact_email` is set it then offers a mailto link instead (`mailtoJoin()` in `js/signup.mjs`). See decision 0012.
- **Look and feel:** `style.css` holds the design tokens on `:root` (one violet accent, cool neutrals, radius `--r*`, shadow `--shadow*`, spacing `--s-*`, type `--t-*`), redefined for dark mode; shared pieces are `.mesh` (soft gradient with a light starfield), `.glass` (frosted card), `.eyebrow`, `.btn`/`.btn.ghost`. The Home hero preview is drawn from the real sample result at load; the Plus phone mock-up is HTML/SVG with invented numbers, labelled illustrative. Motion is limited to `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`.
- **Logo (M14):** `img/logo.svg`: a saucer whose beam lifts three rising bars (finance first, UFO second); `img/favicon.svg` is the same mark drawn for 16 px. The options considered are in `img/logo-options.svg`. `site.json` `logo` sets the path (must be a path inside the site). The header shows the mark and the name "Ur Family Office" with U, F and O in the accent colour (`nameHtml()` in `js/config.mjs`; just "UFO" below 360px wide); the tagline lives in the footer, the hero and the link-preview card. The footer shows the mark, the name and "© 2026" plus `legal_name`. Tab titles use the full name (issues 185, 225). `scripts/build_single_page.py` inlines the logo and favicon as data URIs.
- Shared: `style.css` (one accent, system fonts, light and dark, reduced-motion safe), `js/util.mjs` (helpers, scroll reveal), `js/data.mjs` (loads `../data/*.json` relative to `js/`, so any page depth works, completed months only), `js/result.mjs` (the sample, `runResult()`, headline, next step: shared by Home, Try and the lesson), `js/home.mjs` (Home), `js/app.js` (Try), `js/lesson.mjs` + `js/learn.mjs` (lesson), `js/calc.mjs` (the maths; metrics unchanged, plus the completed-month cutoff).

- `js/calc.mjs` is the maths, `js/app.js` the page. `data/universe.json` lists the tickers offered.
- **Prices:** `scripts/fetch_site_prices.py` writes `data/prices.json` (not committed). The GitHub Actions workflow runs it on every deploy and on weekdays, then publishes `site/` to Pages. Without that file the page uses invented demo prices (`scripts/make_demo_prices.py`). Visitors can also load their own CSV.
- **Copy of the prices:** each workflow run also pushes `prices.json` to the `prices` branch. `scripts/build_single_page.py PRICES OUT.html` builds the **Try page** as one file (style, scripts and prices inlined; the nav links to other pages are dropped).
- **Pages:** published from the public repo https://github.com/simmyhundal/ur-family-office-site (its own workflow fetches prices and deploys). Address: https://simmyhundal.github.io/ur-family-office-site/. Edits here reach it through `.github/workflows/sync-site.yml` (this repo is private).
- **Locally:** `cd site && python -m http.server 8000` (page paths work at the root too, since all links are relative).
- **Tests:** `node --test tests/site/*.test.mjs` and `pytest` (parity with `src/ufo/performance/metrics.py`, and the price builder).
- Information only, not advice.

## Completed months only (M14)
`completedMonths()` in `js/calc.mjs` drops every date whose month has not ended (today's local date); `js/data.mjs` applies it to the bundled prices (and the one-file copy), and Try applies it to a visitor's CSV. Labels say "through Oct 2026" (the last kept month), never the fetch date. Tests: `tests/site/result.test.mjs`.

## Link previews (issue 226)
Every page carries Open Graph and Twitter card tags and an `apple-touch-icon`, written into its `<head>` by `node scripts/sync_site_chrome.mjs` from the page's own `<title>` and description and from `site.json` `base_url` (the public address; a domain change is that one edit plus a sync). The image is `img/og-card.png` (1200x630) and the home-screen icon `img/apple-touch-icon.png` (180x180), both rendered from SVG sources in `scripts/site_images/` by `python scripts/build_site_images.py` (headless Chrome, local). iMessage ignores relative or SVG images, so the URL is absolute and the card is a PNG. iMessage caches previews: test a change with a new query string such as `?v=2`. `tests/site/pages.test.mjs` checks every page.

## Static header and footer
Every page carries the header and footer that `js/chrome.mjs` renders, so pages without JS and the one-file copy match. After editing `site.json` or `js/config.mjs`, run `node scripts/sync_site_chrome.mjs`; a test fails if a page is out of step.

## Sample portfolio and "How Sharpe works"
- **Two invented samples** (`SAMPLES` in `js/result.mjs`), on the real bundled prices (the demo funds when only demo data exists), labelled as samples; editing anything clears the label:
  - **Sample household** (the default: Home hero, `?sample=1` or `?sample=household`): an everyday couple with a 401(k) ($60,000: VTI 50 / VXUS 25 / BND 25), an IRA ($25,000: VOO) and a brokerage account ($15,000: VTI 60 / QQQ 40), added up by value into one mix (VTI 39 / VOO 25 / VXUS 15 / BND 15 / QQQ 6; a test checks the sum), $1,500 a month split the same way. Chosen as the default because the site is now sent to family members with exactly those accounts (issue 234); the banner on Try lists the three accounts.
  - **Sample student** (`?sample=student`, used by the instructor plan): $2,000 to start, $500 a month into VTI 50 / VXUS 20 / BND 20 / QQQ 10.
- **How Sharpe works** (`learn/sharpe/`) runs the loaded mix through the same arithmetic as `docs/lessons/sharpe-ratio.md`, as a beginner lesson: the two Sharpe ratios, then one month's return (worked month highlighted, all months collapsed), minus T-bills, average x12, volatility x sqrt(12), divide (with a rough scale), and "So what?". "Going further" adds the portfolio-theory cards (a 50/50 blend of the mix's two largest funds, Sharpe as a slope, the maximum-Sharpe mix). With a user CSV there is no cash rate, so the steps after the first show n/a.
- The math lives in `calc.mjs` (`sharpeSteps`, `blend5050`, `correlation`, `priceReturns`) with node tests; no new network calls.

## Plus demo (issue 234)
`plus-demo/index.html` is the real Plus dashboard (Overview, Details, Next steps, Learn) for an invented household (Alex and Sam: a joint brokerage, three IRAs and a fixed annuity) on public fund prices, committed as one self-contained page. Rebuild it on the owner's machine (needs the price cache; about two minutes): `python scripts/build_site_plus_demo.py --cache <repo>/.data/prices`, then `python scripts/build_site_images.py` for the Home screenshot (`img/plus-demo-light.png`, `img/plus-demo-dark.png`). The wrapper puts "Demo · invented household" in the sticky header (every tab), links the logo back to the site, adds "Join the list", removes links to the private repo and adds link-preview tags. `tests/test_site_plus_demo.py` checks it holds only the invented household (and, on the owner's machine, none of the private registry's accounts) and makes no network requests. The sync workflow copies all of `site/`, so it is published with the rest.

## Cache-safe deploys (issue 238)
GitHub Pages caches every file for 10 minutes, so a browser could pair new HTML with old scripts. The deployed copy is stamped by `scripts/version_site_assets.py` (run in the public repo's `.github/workflows/site.yml`, whose source is `.github/site-public-workflow.yml` here, after `prices.json` is fetched): every local `href`/`src` in HTML and every relative path string in `.js`/`.mjs` gets `?v=<10 hex of one sha256 over the whole tree>`, and `data.mjs` copies its own stamp onto the data files it fetches. Sources in `site/` stay unstamped. New JS that builds an asset URL dynamically must derive it from `import.meta.url` or use a literal relative path. Nothing above the fold may use `.reveal` or a fade-in (tested in `tests/site/pages.test.mjs`).
