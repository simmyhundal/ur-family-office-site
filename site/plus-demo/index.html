How to read this
✓ Exact
Rests only on today's value and the dated deposits and withdrawals in your files.
~ Estimated
Needs month-end values, which are rebuilt from your history (or taken from a statement where there is one), so it can be a little off.
⚠︎ Does not reconcile
Rebuilt month-end values differ from the account's own statements or snapshots, so it is left out of shorter periods and combined lines rather than shown wrong.
Partial
A combined line that leaves out an account that does not reconcile; the note names it.
▲ ≈ ▼
Excess IRR: ahead of, about even with (within 0.5 percentage points a year) or behind the same money in the benchmark. Sharpe: better than, about the same as (within 0.10) or worse than the benchmark's Sharpe over the same months. Sharpe is compared since inception only.

    All figures are before tax. A taxable account's number is not comparable with an IRA or Roth on an after-tax basis. Transfers between your own accounts are external for each account alone and net out in combined lines. See decision 0011.
