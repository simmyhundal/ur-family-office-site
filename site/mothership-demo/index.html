Fine print
  • This page. An invented family: an adult child and her parents. No real people, accounts or prices. Not wired to data. Information, not investment advice.
  • Who’s included. Each person’s accounts come from their own exports, added with their consent. Turning someone off removes them from every figure.
  • Where the family’s money is.
    • Public markets: brokerage, IRA and 401(k) accounts, as in Plus.
    • The business is the owners’ estimate, never an appraisal by UFO.
    • Real estate counts equity: value minus the mortgage left. Other debts are not modeled here.
    • Bank cash and crypto come from CSV exports; crypto is priced daily from public prices. Hiding them changes the totals on this page only.
  • How the family did. Money-weighted return (IRR) of the public-market accounts since 2016, from dated deposits and withdrawals. Each benchmark gets the same money on the same dates. 60/40 = 60% VTI, 40% BND, rebalanced yearly. The business and homes have their own steps.
  • The studio.
    • Value: the owners’ estimate, picked by method (here revenue × 2; last round price and book value are the other choices).
    • Pay given up = outside pay minus the salary taken, each year since the studio started. It counts as money put in.
    • Cash only counts just the money put in at the start, so it looks far better than it was.
  • Real estate. Value = purchase price moved forward with a public house-price index for the area (for example FHFA); the index here is invented, and the owner can type their own value. Return is on cash put in: down payment, mortgage payments (interest included) and property tax. Rent saved, insurance and repairs are left out.
  • What the advisor costs. Same start ten years ago in both lines, no deposits or withdrawals. Fee of 1% a year taken on the starting balance each year. 60/40 at 0.04% a year in fund costs. Yearly returns are invented. The gap mixes fees and the advisor’s picks; past years don’t predict future ones.

Invented family and figures. Information, not investment or tax advice. UFO is read-only and never moves money.
